## Unexpected Observations

### OBS-001: High Zero Volume Rate

**Observation**: 46.4% of candles have zero volume

**Significance**: 
- Indicates periods with no trading activity
- May affect statistical analysis of volume-based properties
- May indicate exchange-specific behavior (Binance.US)

### OBS-002: Volume Skewness

**Observation**: Volume distribution is extremely right-skewed

**Significance**:
- Most candles have low volume
- Few candles have very high volume
- Mean >> Median

### OBS-003: Trade Count Pattern

**Observation**: Trade count closely follows volume pattern

**Significance**:
- Zero volume candles = zero trades
- High volume candles = high trade count
