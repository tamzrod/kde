## Run Objective

**Objective**: Investigate the relationships between volume and price behavior.

**Why This Investigation**:
- RUN-004 showed volatility transition mechanisms
- RUN-003 showed volume has strong temporal patterns
- Need to understand volume as predictor of price behavior

---

## Methodology

### Volume Classification

| Category | Threshold | Description |
|----------|-----------|-------------|
| ZERO | volume = 0 | No trading |
| LOW | 0 < volume < 0.02 | Minimal trading |
| NORMAL | 0.02 ≤ volume < 0.1 | Typical trading |
| HIGH | volume ≥ 0.1 | Elevated trading |

### Relationship Tests

1. **Volume-Return**: Does high volume predict large returns?
2. **Volume-Volatility**: Is volume correlated with volatility?
3. **Volume-Prediction**: Does volume predict next-period behavior?
4. **Breakout Analysis**: Does high volume precede price breaks?
