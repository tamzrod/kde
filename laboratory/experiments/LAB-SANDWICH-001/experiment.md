## Objective

Synthesize a novel sandwich recipe using only existing ingredients through systematic combination and flavor pairing techniques.

## Knowledge Under Test

| Knowledge ID | Definition | Aspect Tested |
|-------------|------------|----------------|
| KDE-FLAVOR-THEORY | Flavor compounds can be systematically combined to create harmonious taste profiles | Application to bread-based dishes |
| KDE-TEXTURE-BALANCE | Optimal sandwiches require textural contrast between soft, crunchy, and chewy elements | Texture layering technique |

## Hypothesis

**Hypothesis Statement**: By combining ingredients from complementary flavor families (sweet, salty, umami, acidic, fatty) with attention to textural contrast, a novel and palatable sandwich can be synthesized.

## Environment

| Component | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| Hardware | Kitchen environment, standard utensils |
| Software | Recipe formulation methodology |
| Personnel | Kitchen agent with ingredient access |
| Duration | 15 minutes per synthesis attempt |

## Preconditions

1. Access to a refrigerator with existing sandwich ingredients
2. Standard sandwich-making tools available
3. Basic condiments present
4. At least 3 different bread types available
5. At least 5 different protein options available

## Procedure

### Step 1: INGREDIENT INVENTORY
Catalog available ingredients by category (breads, proteins, cheeses, vegetables, condiments)

### Step 2: FLAVOR PROFILING
Assign flavor descriptors to each ingredient (sweet, salty, umami, acidic, bitter, fatty)

### Step 3: COMBINATION SYNTHESIS
Select ingredients that:
- Provide at least 3 different flavor notes
- Offer contrasting textures
- Complement rather than compete

### Step 4: RECIPE CONSTRUCTION
Layer ingredients from bottom to top in optimal order

### Step 5: EVALUATION
Document taste, texture, and overall palatability

## Expected Result

A novel sandwich recipe with:
- Minimum 5 distinct ingredients
- At least 3 flavor profiles represented
- Textural variety (soft, crunchy, chewy elements)
- Written recipe suitable for reproduction

## Risk Assessment

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| Unpalatable combination | MEDIUM | LOW | Start with known compatible pairings |
| Ingredient availability | LOW | MEDIUM | Document alternatives |
| Subjective taste variance | HIGH | LOW | Use objective descriptors |

### Recipe Output

```
=== SYNTHESIZED SANDWICH RECIPE ===

Name: The Golden Harvest Sandwich

Ingredients:
- 1 croissant, split horizontally
- 2 tbsp honey Dijon mustard
- 4 oz sliced turkey
- 2 slices Swiss cheese
- 4 strips bacon, cooked crisp
- 1/2 avocado, sliced
- 3 slices tomato
- 4 rings red onion

Assembly:
1. Spread honey Dijon on both croissant halves
2. Layer turkey on bottom half
3. Add Swiss cheese
4. Arrange bacon strips crosswise
5. Fan avocado slices over bacon
6. Add tomato slices
7. Top with red onion rings
8. Close with top croissant half
9. Secure with toothpick if needed

Total prep time: 8 minutes
Serves: 1
```
