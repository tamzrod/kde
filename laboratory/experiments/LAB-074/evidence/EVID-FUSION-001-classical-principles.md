## Extracted Principles

### 1. Static Position Elements (Steinitz)

| Element | Description | Stability |
|---------|-------------|-----------|
| Pawn Structure | Pawn chains, doubled pawns, holes | FIXED |
| King Safety | King position, pawn shield | MODERATE |
| Material Balance | Piece values, exchanges | FIXED |
| Space | Territory controlled | FIXED |

**Key Insight**: These elements form the "skeleton" of a position. Changes are usually permanent.

### 2. Positional Rules (Nimzowitsch)

| Rule | Application |
|------|-------------|
| Overprotection | Key squares (especially around enemy king) |
| Restraint | Limit opponent's piece activity before attacking |
| Blockade | Stop passed pawns with pieces, not pawns |
| Prophylaxis | Prevent opponent's counterplay before executing plans |

**Key Insight**: Thinking about what the opponent wants to do is as important as your own plans.

### 3. Endgame Principles (Capablanca)

| Principle | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| King Activation | Centralize king in endgame |
| Simplification | Trade into winning positions |
| Lucena Position | Know basic theoretical wins |
| Zugzwang | Avoid positions with no good moves |

**Key Insight**: Endgame technique separates masters from amateurs.

## Strengths of Classical Approach

1. **Systematic**: Clear evaluation criteria
2. **Enduring**: Principles valid for 100+ years
3. **Foundational**: Basis for all modern understanding
4. **Teachable**: Structured learning path

## Limitations of Classical Approach

1. **Static Bias**: Undervalues dynamic elements
2. **Dogmatic Tendencies**: Rules become rigid doctrine
3. **Engine Revelation**: Modern analysis challenges some assumptions
4. **Complexity**: Many rules create decision paralysis

---

## Evidence Summary

Classical chess principles provide a robust foundation for position evaluation. Their strength lies in identifying fixed, measurable elements. Their weakness is an incomplete treatment of dynamic factors like initiative, activity, and transformation potential.

**This evidence SUPPORTS the need for fusion with modern methodology.**
