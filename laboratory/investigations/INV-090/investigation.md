## Research Question

Can we synthesize a unified chess technique that fuses the principles of classical chess (positional play, endgame mastery) with modern chess methodology (engine-informed analysis, dynamic tactics, universal systems) to create a cohesive approach that leverages the strengths of both eras?

## Scope

### Included
- Analysis of classical chess principles (Steinitz, Nimzowitsch, Capablanca)
- Integration of modern engine-informed techniques
- Synthesis of tactical and positional playstyles
- Development of a unified decision framework

### Excluded
- Opening memorization research (covered in LAB-062)
- Psychological aspects of chess (time pressure, tilt)
- Tournament preparation logistics

## Background

### Classical Chess Heritage
Classical chess emphasizes:
- Positional principles (pawn structure, space, piece coordination)
- Endgame mastery as foundation
- Prophylactic thinking
- Restraint of opponent's counterplay

### Modern Chess Evolution
Modern chess incorporates:
- Engine analysis revealing previously underestimated positions
- Dynamic piece play over static advantages
- Universal opening systems (不用记忆大量变例)
- Practical chances over theoretical perfection

### The Synthesis Opportunity
Both methodologies have proven successful at the highest levels. The hypothesis is that a fused technique combining positional understanding with dynamic awareness could provide superior practical results.

## Hypothesis

**Primary Hypothesis**: A fused chess technique that integrates classical positional principles with modern dynamic tactics will outperform either methodology in isolation for players rated 1200-1800 ELO.

**Secondary Hypothesis**: The fusion point lies in treating positional advantages as dynamic resources that enable tactical opportunities, rather than as static evaluations.
