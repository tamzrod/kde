The author presents a **pedagogical pivot**: shifting from definition-memorization to problem-solving context.

**Before (Failed Approach):**

**After (Proposed Approach):**

| Concept | Problem Framing |
|---------|-----------------|

### Key Insight

## Methodology Observation

The investigation notes the author uses **Socratic questioning**:

> "Kapag gumagawa ka ba ng system, iniisip mo muna ang definitions ng OOP? O iniisip mo muna kung paano mo sosolusyonan ang problema?"

This technique:
- Engages metacognition
- Surfaces existing mental models
- Creates cognitive dissonance before resolution
