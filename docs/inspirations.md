# Inspirations

The conceptual foundations that shaped KDE.

---

## Conceptual Themes

KDE draws from multiple traditions:

| Theme | Source | Contribution |
|-------|--------|--------------|
| Scientific Method | Research | Evidence-based reasoning |
| Laboratory | Academia | Systematic investigation |
| Mission Control | Aerospace | Orchestration and coordination |
| Industrial Control | Engineering | Policy enforcement |

---

## Scientific Method

**From**: Traditional research methodology

**Adapted for KDE**:
- Hypothesis formation
- Experimental validation
- Evidence collection
- Iterative refinement

---

## Laboratory

**From**: Academic research labs

**Adapted for KDE**:
- Investigation structure
- Evidence documentation
- Peer review process
- Reproducibility standards

---

## Mission Control

**From**: NASA space missions

**Adapted for KDE**:
- Pre-flight checks
- Mission readiness
- Go/No-Go decisions
- Orchestration terminology

---

## Industrial Control

**From**: Control systems engineering

**Adapted for KDE**:
- Policy enforcement
- State machine transitions
- Error handling
- Safety interlocks

---

## Why Hybrid?

No single metaphor captures KDE's dual nature:

| Aspect | Preferred Theme |
|--------|-----------------|
| Knowledge operations | Scientific Laboratory |
| Governance operations | Mission Control |
| Policy enforcement | Industrial Control |
