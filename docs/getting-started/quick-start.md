# Quick Start Guide

**Get up to speed with KDE in 5 minutes**

---

## One-Line Summary

**KDE is a way to use AI for research while keeping humans in control of what gets approved.**

---

## What You Need to Know

### KDE Has Two Parts

| Part | Who Does It | What Happens |
|------|-------------|--------------|
| **Research** | AI | Investigates, gathers evidence, analyzes |
| **Decisions** | Human | Approves work, promotes knowledge |

### The Golden Rule

> **AI investigates. Humans decide.**

---

## The KDE Workflow (Simple)

```
1. Human: "Find best practices for API security"
          ↓
2. AI: Gathers research, collects evidence
          ↓
3. AI: "Here are findings with sources"
          ↓
4. Human: Reviews evidence, approves or asks for changes
          ↓
5. Human: "This is now official knowledge"
```

---

## Key Concepts (30 Seconds Each)

### Concept 1: Evidence is Required

**Rule**: Every claim must have a source.

❌ Bad: "REST APIs are better than GraphQL"
✅ Good: "According to the 2024 API Survey, 67% of companies prefer REST"

### Concept 2: Humans Approve

**Rule**: AI cannot approve its own work.

- AI can investigate
- AI can write findings
- Only a human can say "this is good enough to be knowledge"

### Concept 3: Knowledge Has Stages

**Draft** → **Validated** → **Promoted**

| Stage | Who Approves | Meaning |
|-------|--------------|---------|
| Draft | Nobody | AI's initial work |
| Validated | Human reviewer | Evidence checked |
| Promoted | Human authority | Official knowledge |

---

## Common Terms (Quick Definitions)

| Term | Plain English |
|------|--------------|
| **Engine** | The method AI uses to investigate |
| **Seed** | Starting principles that don't change |
| **Investigation** | A research project |
| **Evidence** | Proof (sources, data, citations) |
| **Promoted** | Made official by humans |

---

## Example of KDE in Action

### Situation

A developer asks: "What authentication method should I use for my API?"

### Without KDE

AI says: "Use OAuth 2.0, it's the best."
- No sources
- No explanation
- No accountability

### With KDE

**AI Investigation:**
> "Based on:
> - OWASP Guidelines (2024)
> - API Survey of 500 companies
> - Security audit findings
>
> OAuth 2.0 is recommended for:
> - Third-party integrations
> - User authentication
> - Token-based access"

**Human Review:**
- Check: Are these sources credible?
- Check: Is the reasoning sound?
- Check: Is anything missing?

**Human Approval:**
> "Approved. This is now knowledge."

---

## What KDE is NOT

| Misconception | Reality |
|---------------|---------|
| "AI does everything" | Humans approve all decisions |
| "Any AI output is knowledge" | Only validated evidence becomes knowledge |
| "No rules needed" | Five principles govern the process |
| "Just ChatGPT with extra steps" | Structured methodology with accountability |

---

## Your Role in KDE

Depending on your position:

| Role | What You Do |
|------|-------------|
| **Questioner** | Ask questions, define scope |
| **Reviewer** | Check evidence, validate findings |
| **Approver** | Make final decisions on promotion |
| **Contributor** | Participate in research |
