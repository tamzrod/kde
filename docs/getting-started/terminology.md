# Terminology Glossary

**Plain-language definitions of KDE terms**

---

## A

### Approval / Approved

**What it means**: A human has reviewed and accepted work as valid.

**In plain English**: A person said "yes, this is good enough."

**Related**: [Validation](#v)

---

### Artifact

**What it means**: Any document, file, or record created during research.

**In plain English**: Anything produced by the work (reports, findings, data).

---

## C

### Candidate

**What it means**: A state meaning "ready for consideration but not yet approved."

**In plain English**: "This looks good, but someone needs to check it."

**See also**: [Promoted](#p), [Validated](#v)

---

### Claim

**What it means**: A statement that something is true.

**In plain English**: "X is true" or "X should be done."

**Note**: In KDE, every claim needs evidence to support it.

---

## D

### Draft

**What it means**: Initial work produced by AI, not yet reviewed.

**In plain English**: "First version, needs checking."

**See also**: [Promoted](#p), [Validated](#v)

---

## E

### Engine

**What it means**: A defined methodology for how research should be conducted.

**In plain English**: The step-by-step method AI follows to investigate.

**Examples**:
- **Beta**: Context-aware investigation
- **Gamma**: Causal analysis (finding causes)
- **Delta**: Reproducible research

**Related**: [Seed](#s), [Methodology](#m)

---

### Evidence

**What it means**: Proof that supports a claim.

**In plain English**: Sources, data, citations, or facts that back up what you're saying.

**Examples**:
- "According to the 2024 Security Survey..."
- "The documentation states..."
- "Data from 500 companies shows..."

**Related**: [Claim](#c), [Inference](#i)

---

### Experiment

**What it means**: A specific test or investigation within the larger research process.

**In plain English**: A focused piece of research with a specific question.

---

## G

### Governance

**What it means**: The system of rules and policies that keeps KDE working correctly.

**In plain English**: "Who decides what" and "what are the rules."

**Related**: [Approval](#a), [Human Oversight](#h)

---

## H

### Human Oversight

**What it means**: The requirement that humans review and approve AI work.

**In plain English**: "Humans check what AI does."

**Related**: [Approval](#a), [Promoted](#p)

---

## I

### Inference

**What it means**: A conclusion drawn from evidence.

**In plain English**: "Based on the facts, I conclude..."

**Note**: This is different from evidence. Evidence is facts. Inference is interpretation.

**Example**:
- **Evidence**: "90% of companies use OAuth"
- **Inference**: "OAuth is becoming the standard"

**Related**: [Evidence](#e), [Hypothesis](#h)

---

### Investigation

**What it means**: A complete research project to answer a question.

**In plain English**: A structured search for knowledge.

**Related**: [Experiment](#e), [Workflow](#w)

---

## K

### Kernel

**What it means**: The core principles that don't change.

**In plain English**: The fundamental rules that everything else is built on.

---

## L

### Laboratory

**What it means**: The environment where KDE research happens.

**In plain English**: Where the research is conducted.

**Contains**:
- Questions to investigate
- Investigations (research projects)
- Evidence and findings
- Governance rules

---

## M

### Methodology

**What it means**: The system of methods used for research.

**In plain English**: How you do the research step by step.

**Related**: [Engine](#e)

---

## O

### Oversight

**What it means**: Human supervision of AI work.

**In plain English**: Humans watching and approving what AI does.

**See**: [Human Oversight](#h)

---

## P

### Promote / Promoted

**What it means**: Making validated work "official" knowledge.

**In plain English**: "This is now approved knowledge that can be trusted."

**Requirements**: Human approval required

**See also**: [Draft](#d), [Validated](#v), [Approval](#a)

---

## Q

### Question

**What it means**: A knowledge gap or inquiry that needs investigation.

**In plain English**: Something we don't know and need to research.

---

## R

### Research

**What it means**: Systematic investigation to discover knowledge.

**In plain English**: Looking for answers with evidence.

---

## S

### Seed

**What it means**: A foundational principle that guides all research.

**In plain English**: Starting rules that don't change.

**Current Seeds**:
- **Genesis**: The five oversight rules
- **Evolution**: How knowledge grows
- **Bootstrap**: How to start research

**Related**: [Engine](#e), [Kernel](#k)

---

### Source

**What it means**: Where evidence comes from.

**In plain English**: The origin of your proof (study, document, data, etc.)

---

## T

### Tool

**What it means**: A capability or resource used in research.

**In plain English**: Something that helps do the work.

---

## V

### Validate / Validated

**What it means**: Evidence has been checked and confirmed.

**In plain English**: "Someone verified the sources and they check out."

**Note**: This is different from Promoted. Validated = evidence checked. Promoted = made official.

**Related**: [Draft](#d), [Promoted](#p)

---

## W

### Workflow

**What it means**: The sequence of steps in KDE research.

**In plain English**: The order things happen.

**Typical workflow**:
1. Question asked
2. Investigation started
3. Evidence gathered
4. Findings documented
5. Human reviews
6. Knowledge promoted
