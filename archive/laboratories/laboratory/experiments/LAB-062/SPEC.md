# LAB-062 Specification

## Experiment Design

### Independent Variables
- **Knowledge Domain**: Openings, Tactics, Endgames, Strategy
- **Learning Method**: Focused (Pareto) vs. Comprehensive

### Dependent Variables
- **Practical Coverage**: % of situations adequately handled
- **Rating Efficiency**: ELO gain per hour of study
- **Retention Rate**: Knowledge retained over time

### Controlled Variables
- **Study Time**: Fixed hours per domain
- **Player Level**: Beginner to Intermediate (800-1400 ELO)
- **Material**: Same resources across methods

### Measurement Instruments
- Position coverage analysis (simulated)
- Time-to-solve metrics
- Knowledge retention surveys

---

## Validation Criteria

1. PARETO-CHESS achieves ≥75% practical coverage
2. Top 20 tactical patterns cover ≥85% of opportunities
3. Essential endgames cover ≥90% of practical endings
4. Universal openings cover ≥60% of game types
