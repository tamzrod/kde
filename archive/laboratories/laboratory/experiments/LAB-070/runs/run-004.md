## Name Analysis

### 1. Pre-digested (Winner)

**Concept**: Food that's processed before eating

**Why it fits**:
- ✅ AI receives "ready-to-use" data (no parsing needed)
- ✅ Implies transformation from raw to usable
- ✅ Clear metaphor for pre-processing
- ✅ Unique, memorable term

**Why not others**:
- ⚠️ Unusual for software (but effective)

### 2. Compiled Artifact (Runner-up)

**Concept**: Like compiled vs interpreted code

**Why it fits**:
- ✅ Builds on LAB-069 "compiler" terminology
- ✅ Implies transformation step
- ✅ Professional, technical

**Why lower**:
- ⚠️ "Artifact" is vague
- ⚠️ Less intuitive than Pre-digested

### 3. Indexed Path

**Concept**: Book index for fast lookup

**Why it fits**:
- ✅ Implies O(1) fast access
- ✅ Familiar concept

**Why lower**:
- ⚠️ Suggests search/indexing
- ⚠️ Doesn't convey pre-processing
