## Methodology

### Unified State Definition

Combine three dimensions into market states:

| Dimension | Low | Medium | High |
|-----------|-----|--------|------|
| Return | <-0.05% | -0.05% to 0.05% | >0.05% |
| Volatility | <0.02% | 0.02% to 0.08% | >0.08% |
| Volume | Zero | <0.05 BTC | ≥0.05 BTC |

This creates 27 possible combinations (3×3×3), but many are rare.

### State Simplification

For analysis, simplify to dominant patterns:

| State | Characteristics |
|-------|-----------------|
| QUIET | Low volatility, low volume |
| TRENDING_UP | Positive return, elevated vol |
| TRENDING_DOWN | Negative return, elevated vol |
| VOLATILE | High volatility, any direction |
| UNCERTAIN | Mixed signals |

### Sequence Analysis

Look for recurring 2-state and 3-state sequences.

## Combined Mechanism Hypothesis

### HYPOTHESIS H1: Volatility Momentum Hypothesis

**Statement**: High-volatility periods exhibit directional persistence, but eventually revert to choppy consolidation.

**Evidence**:
1. VOL_UP self-transition: 22% (3x random)
2. VOL_DN self-transition: 25% (3x random)
3. Mean volatile duration: 2 minutes
4. CHOPPY is universal attractor

**Mechanism**:
```
HIGH VOLATILITY → DIRECTIONAL CONTINUATION (brief) → CHOPPY CONSOLIDATION
     ↑                                              ↓
     └────────────── REVERSION ←─────────────────────┘
```

**Confidence**: MEDIUM (7 days of data)

**Limitations**:
- 7-day sample may not generalize
- Binance.US-specific patterns
- Short volatile episodes

**Confounding Factors**:
- Market news/events not controlled
- Sample period may be non-representative

**Falsification Experiments**:
- Test on longer time series
- Test on different exchanges
- Test during different market conditions
