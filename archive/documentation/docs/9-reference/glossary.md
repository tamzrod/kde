# Glossary

---

## A

### AI

Artificial Intelligence. In KDE, AI operates under human authority as an investigation assistant.

### Approval

Human authorization for a document or action. AI cannot approve its own work.

---

## B

### Bootstrap

The initialization process that prepares KDE for operation. Includes gate verification.

### Bootstrap Gates

Pre-flight checks that verify system readiness before work begins.

---

## C

### Capability

A specific ability or skill. Engines expose capabilities; requests require capabilities.

### Consensus

Agreement among multiple Engines on a result or conclusion.

---

## D

### Document State

The current status of a document in its lifecycle (DRAFT, REVIEW, APPROVED, etc.).

---

## E

### ECU

Execution Control Unit. The orchestration component that coordinates runtime operations.

### Engine

A reasoning methodology implementation. Defines how investigations are conducted.

### Evidence

Documented facts from verifiable sources. The foundation of KDE claims.

### Experiment

A systematic test designed to validate or refute a hypothesis.

---

## F

### Frozen

A status indicating immutability. Frozen Seeds cannot be modified.

---

## G

### Governance

The system of rules and human oversight that governs KDE operations.

---

## H

### Human Authority

The principle that humans make final decisions in KDE. AI operates under human direction.

---

## I

### Inference

A conclusion logically derived from evidence. Distinguished from evidence and hypothesis.

### Investigation

A systematic inquiry following KDE methodology.

### Iteration

The process of repeating a cycle with improvements based on results.

---

## K

### KDE

Knowledge Discovery Engine. An evidence-based research methodology.

### Knowledge

Validated understanding that has been approved and promoted.

---

## L

### Laboratory

The KDE workspace where investigations are conducted.

---

## M

### Meta-Analysis

Analysis of KDE itself. Studying how KDE works to improve KDE.

---

## P

### Policy Layer

The component that enforces governance rules at runtime.

### Principle

A fundamental rule governing KDE behavior.

### Promotion

Moving validated knowledge to the /knowledge/ directory. Requires human approval.

---

## R

### Runtime

The executing KDE system, including ECU, Engines, and Seeds.

---

## S

### Scientific Loop

The cycle of Observe → Hypothesize → Predict → Test → Analyze → Iterate.

### Seed

Immutable foundational principles that guide reasoning.

### State Machine

A system defining valid states and transitions for documents or processes.

---

## T

### Traceability

The ability to trace a conclusion back to its supporting evidence.

---

## V

### Validation

The process of testing and verifying claims before promotion.
