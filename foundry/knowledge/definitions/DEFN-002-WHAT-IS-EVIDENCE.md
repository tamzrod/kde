## Definition

**Evidence** is verifiable information that supports or refutes a knowledge claim.

---

## Components

| Component | Definition |
|-----------|------------|
| **Verifiable** | Can be confirmed or refuted |
| **Information** | Processed, not raw data |
| **Support/Refute** | Can be positive or negative |
| **Knowledge Claim** | Must relate to a specific claim |

---

## Evidence Types

| Type | Description | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Direct** | Directly observed | "The pump failed at 10,000 hours" |
| **Documented** | From authoritative source | IEEE standard specification |
| **Experimental** | From controlled tests | LAB-023 experiment results |
| **Field** | From practice | Industry case study |

---

## Evidence Requirements

### Must Have
- Source citation
- Verifiable claim
- Clear relation to knowledge claim

### Must Not Have
- Unverifiable assertions
- Hearsay without citation
- Mixed evidence/inference

---

## Evidence vs Related Concepts

| Concept | vs. Evidence |
|---------|-------------|
| **Data** | Raw, unprocessed facts |
| **Information** | Organized but unverified |
| **Opinion** | Personal view without verification |
| **Inference** | Conclusion drawn from evidence |

---

## Evidence Quality

| Quality | Criteria |
|---------|----------|
| **Strong** | Direct, verified, cited |
| **Moderate** | Documented, verifiable |
| **Weak** | Inferential, uncited |
